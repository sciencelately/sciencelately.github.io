Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Science Lately ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and our internal video publishing tool (the "Science Lately Publisher"), which uses YouTube API Services.

YouTube API Services

The Science Lately Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Science Lately YouTube channel. By using any YouTube feature connected to Science Lately, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the Publisher

The Publisher is an internal tool. It is used only by the Science Lately channel owner to publish to the Science Lately channel. It is not offered to the public, and members of the public cannot sign in to it.

Information we access and why

  • Google account authorization (OAuth token): when the channel owner signs in with Google, the Publisher receives an access token with the youtube.upload permission. It is used only to upload our own videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and confirm the upload succeeded.
  • Upload results: the YouTube video ID and upload status returned by the API are recorded in a local log so the same video is never uploaded twice.

The Publisher does not access, collect or store data about YouTube viewers or any other YouTube users, and does not read comments, subscribers, analytics or any other channel's data.

How information is stored and shared

The authorization token and upload logs are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by us, and we do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties. Information is shared with Google/YouTube only as needed to upload videos through the YouTube API.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The Publisher does not place cookies or similar technologies on any user's device.

Retention and deletion

The authorization token is kept until access is revoked or the channel owner signs out of the tool, at which point it is deleted. Upload logs are kept for as long as the corresponding videos remain published, then deleted.

Access granted to Science Lately can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services).

Children's privacy

Our tool does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date above.
